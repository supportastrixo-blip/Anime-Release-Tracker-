Anime Release Tracker - Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026
Who we are
- Anime Release Tracker ("the app") shows real release dates and live countdowns for
anime, and lets signed-in users react, review, chat and save a personal list. This policy
explains what information the app uses and why.
Information we collect
- Account: your email address, a username you choose and your password. Passwords
are handled and stored (hashed) by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
- Optional profile picture: if you choose one, the image is resized on your device and
uploaded. Profile pictures can be viewed by anyone who has the picture link.
- Content you create: reviews, ratings, reactions, your saved list, chat messages, and
reports or blocks you make.
- On your device only: your language choice and the release reminders you set. These
are not sent to our servers.
Microphone and voice input
- If you tap the microphone in Community chat, the app asks for microphone permission.
Speech is turned into text by the speech-recognition service of your Android device
(usually provided by Google). The app does not record or store audio. The text only
appears in the message box and is sent only if you press Send.
Notifications and reminders
- If you allow notifications, the app can show reminders that you set on an anime
countdown. Reminders are scheduled on your own device. The app does not send push
notifications from a server. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone
settings.
Photos
- The app only accesses the single photo you pick when you choose a profile picture. It
does not scan or upload your gallery. Advertising
- The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device
information such as your advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads, as
described in Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your
Android settings.
Third-party services
- Supabase: hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
- AniList: provides anime information. Your device contacts AniList directly to load it, so
AniList can see your IP address like any website you visit.
- Google AdMob: advertising (see above).
What other users can see
- Signed-in users can see your username, profile picture, reviews, ratings, reaction totals
and chat messages. Your email address and saved list are private to you.
How we use information
- To run the app's features, show community content, keep the community safe (reports
and blocking), show ads, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.
Keeping and deleting data
- We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your own reviews and chat
messages inside the app, remove your profile picture, and cancel reminders. To ask for
your account and related data to be deleted, contact us using the email below.
Security
- Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to each table is restricted so users
can only change their own data. No system is completely secure, so please use a
strong, unique password.
Children
- The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do
not create an account if you are under that age. If you believe a child has given us
personal information, contact us and we will remove it. Changes to this policy
- We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always available
inside the app under Profile > Privacy policy.
Contact
